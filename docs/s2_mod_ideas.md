# Season 2 (Ep 13-25) mod ideas

## (a) Mikiko skills/forms
- Fire Blitz: fire projectile (Ep 13)
- Blazing Wind: fire+wind vortex that burns and suffocates a target, high Karma cost (Ep 13)
- HekuBlade scatter + Hekubarrier: ring of blades that becomes a near-unbreakable barrier (Ep 13)
- True empathy: copy the last attack a mob/boss used on you; overuse gives a "withdrawal" debuff (Ep 13, 25)
- Netsu / Netsu Burst: speed burst with fire trail; can be cast on an ally (burns them) (Ep 13-14, 25)
- Xeno Blast: big beam, stronger as a combo with Rhalix (Ep 13, 18, 25)
- Wind flight: creative-style flight that drains Karma (Ep 18, 22)
- Soul Splitter: invisible soul-clone that attacks (Ep 18)
- Fox King mode: timed buff that also highlights mobs/players by scent (Ep 13, 14, 22)
- Corrupted form: rage transformation, dark blade + corrupted Karma blast, sees invisible mobs, health drains / heart-stop risk (Ep 20-21)
- Manifest Greatness: X-arms gesture that clears debuffs and turns him red (XL buff) (Ep 22, 25)
- Kaeru!: summon Kaeru as a companion from the Hekublade (Ep 22)
- Eating Skylander wings: item that heals and raises Karma (Ep 25)

## (b) Rhalix skills/forms
- Kishi: gravity slow/pin; also slows projectiles in an area (Ep 14, 25)
- Wing retract: drop wings to break a grab / heal wings (Ep 13-14)
- Lunar form: night-only transformation (silver Karma, crescent eyes, moons on body), stronger under moonlight (Ep 23)
- Lunar trials questline: butterfly room, frozen underwater escape (find the light), memory trial (Ep 23)
- Moon reflect: reflects projectiles, costs Karma (Ep 23)
- Moon Fall: drop a moon on an area (Ep 25)
- Karma activation: unlock Karma for another player (risky) (Ep 18, 21)
- Soul Splitter (Ep 23)

## (c) NPCs / bosses
- Ultima boss: power phases 10/25/45/70/100%, blasts, flight, predicts attacks, endless stamina; defeat by holding him ~15 s with the antivirus (Ep 13, 19-25)
- Kyle boss: invisibility (more hits = more visible), speed, Clone Clone splitting clones, KA steal on touch (Ep 17, 20-24)
- Undead Technica mini-boss: winged corpse in Attack Mode; ripping her wings is the weak point (Ep 20-21)
- Zane: water magic that turns into quicksand traps (Ep 21)
- Logan (Thunder flash, portal escape) and Lexy(Persephone) (power kicks) as SR enemies (Ep 22)
- Zack: demon ally/rival with Demon's Eye and one-punch KO (Ep 22, 24)
- Ai Oni: demon who opens demon portals (Ep 24)
- Royal Sisters as allies: Kyrie (Kishi), Atherea (Earth), Ranrea (tank) (Ep 22)
- Yuri Tenjo: trainer/vendor at HQ (VR training, antivirus, healing pods) (Ep 15-25)
- Sam (Psychotic time bomb bat) and Sintrie (Vampire cut knife, potions) as SP allies (Ep 14, 19)
- Kaeru: Hekublade guardian with Fox Heal (revive) and scent tracking (Ep 22)
- Demon/gas-patient mobs; Skyhater "collector" mobs that target Skylanders' wings (Ep 16, 24, 25)

- Hekublade: form changes with the holder; useless shell without Kaeru (Ep 13, 22)
- Psychotic time bomb bat (Sam) and Vampire cut knife (Sintrie) (Ep 14, 19)
