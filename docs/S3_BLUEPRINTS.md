# Season 3 character blueprints (Eps 26-31)

Extracted from the S3 script. Skills marked (in mod) already exist in Xeno Warriors.

## Mikiko
**Role:** Demigod hero, leader of the Xeno Warriors ("AmenXL Mikiko"); Orenji clan; student of Komatsu Norio; ally.
**Look:** A boy with a girl's name. Arm burned and bandaged after the Ultima battle (Ep 26). Wears the Fox King crown in Fox King mode (lost in Ep 29); has a work outfit for Kitsune's Farm. Nicknamed "Red Mikikies".
**Forms:** Base form (Komatsu says it is as strong as his old Fox King mode.); Fox King mode (Uses the Fox King crown; only works for a Fox Kin. Lost when Chase took the crown (Ep 29).); Corrupted state (His soul state is corrupted; anger triggers a transformation he once could not reverse (Ep 26).); Glowing red (XL) (Glows red after chanting "Manifest True Greatness" (Ep 27).)
**Weapons:** Hekublade (Heku), Hekubeats (headphone form), Fox King crown (lost)
- **True empathy** (ep 26): Says what he feels and copies others' moves; used it to pull off the Dance Kick. Overuse knocked him out for 2 weeks, and Komatsu warns it can make him lose his true self.
- **Dance Kick** (ep 26): Komatsu Norio's signature kick, copied with True empathy.
- **Karma sensing** (in mod) (ep 26): Senses how much Karma someone has, and dark Karma nearby or far off (Royal capital).
- **Manifest Greatness / Manifest True Greatness** (ep 27): A chant. Said with true belief, it powers him up and makes him glow red.
- **Netsu** (in mod) (ep 27): Burst to top speed while running.
- **Netsu Bust** (ep 27): All-out speed burst; broke the treadmill.
- **Heku** (in mod) (ep 31): Summons the Hekublade (looked different and stronger in Ep 31); sent all the C-souls into the blade.
- **Kaeru return!** (ep 28): Calls Kaeru back into the Hekublade.
- **Hekubeats** (ep 30): Kaeru turns the Hekublade into headphones so he can hear her and fight to the music.
- **Two-finger catch** (ep 31): Stopped Zack's knife with two fingers.
- **Rap combat** (ep 31): Dodges Zack's attacks while rapping to the beat, in Komatsu Norio style.

## Rhalix
**Role:** Rhalix Murasaki of the Murasaki clan; captain of the Sky Patrol (SP), ex royal knight; becomes King of Royal capital Section 1; ally.
**Look:** His eyes glow bright red when he is enraged (Ep 26). Kyrie restyles his hair as a disguise after the 2-year timeskip (Ep 27).
**Forms:** Lunar form (A new form, mentioned by Mikiko (Ep 26, 28).)
**Weapons:** Knife made from his wires
- **Kshi** (in mod) (ep 26): Gravity-element power that pins enemies so they can't move; also used to break apart Keita's KA.
- **Gravity element** (in mod) (ep 26): The queens identify his power as a Gravity element.
- **KA Release** (ep 29): Breaks free of an enemy's KA.
- **Wire knife** (ep 29): Makes a knife out of his wires to cut Keita.
- **Flight** (in mod) (ep 26): Flies between locations.
- **Karma sensing** (in mod) (ep 29): Senses enemies' Karma nearby.

## Sintrie (Max)
**Role:** Real name Sintrie, goes by Max; Sky Patrol fighter from the commoner islands; once tied to SR by a debt; ally.
**Look:** White hair (Lexy jokes he's old as the dinosaurs). Flashback: light yellow and gray outfit, light blue wings. Smokes heavily.
- **Wind of Sorrow** (ep 28): His wind element; also how he travels fast.
- **Vampire Slice** (ep 28): His KA, a slashing strike. He calls the Ep 28 one the 2nd version and says he has reached level 3.
- **KA Release** (ep 28): Breaks Keita's mind-control KA.
- **Rapid Vampire Slice** (ep 30): Rapid slashes that drain the target's blood and can't be removed by KA Release.
- **Vampire cut (healing)** (ep 31): His KA heals him; closed the sword wound through his chest.
- **Flight** (ep 28): Flies using his wings.

## Sam
**Role:** Sky Patrol knight ("SP Knight Samatha" on his ID), one of SP's strongest fighters; ally.
**Look:** Green hair. Wears a Karma reducing hoodie.
**Weapons:** Bat, Karma reducing hoodie
- **Clear Space** (ep 29): Teleports the target to right in front of him, or himself to them.
- **Batter up!** (ep 29): Bat swing that sends the enemy flying.
- **PTB psychotic time bomb** (ep 29): Plants bombs that go off inside the target after a 10-second countdown; killed Logan.
- **Batter batter and shoot** (ep 29): Finishing bat combo, used together with PTB.

## Lexy
**Role:** Sky Patrol member taken by SR, turned into a demon Skylander and fused with Persephone; ally.
**Look:** Wore a mask and a Karma reducing hoodie at SR, then gave them up (Ep 27). No longer shy after 2 years.
**Forms:** Demon Skylander / Persephone fusion (Shares a body with Persephone and resists fully fusing with her.); Lexy(Persephone) (Persephone takes control of her body (Ep 28).)
**Weapons:** Karma reducing hoodie (given up), Mask
- **High Karma** (ep 27): Sam says her Karma is off the charts.
- **Flight** (ep 27): Flies to the Royal capital.

## Persephone
**Role:** Demon fused with Lexy and loyal to SR; enemy, though she bonds with Lexy.
**Forms:** Lexy(Persephone) (Controls Lexy's body.)
- **Take over** (ep 28): Takes control of Lexy's body.
- **Royal capital knowledge** (ep 27): Knows where the Royal capital is.

## Komatsu Norio
**Role:** "Master Matsu", the self-styled genuine and passionate ultimate disco dancer and rapper; runs a disco and trains Mikiko and the gang; ally.
**Look:** Disco dancer; he is getting new disco outfits for the group (Ep 31).
- **Dance Kick** (ep 26): "The Komatsu Norio! Dance Kick!", his signature move.
- **Speed** (ep 26): Fast enough to keep tripping and kicking Mikiko.
- **Karma teaching** (ep 26): Teaches Karma through belief, certainty and the Komatsu Norio style of life.
- **Treadmill-breaking sprint** (ep 27): Broke the treadmill to beat Mikiko's speed record.

## Stevie
**Role:** "Purple Stevie", gang member, rapper and Komatsu Norio's student; ally.
- **Karma control** (ep 26): Learned Karma a month before Ep 26; knocked Mikiko out of Fox King mode.
- **Dance Kick** (ep 26): Hit Mikiko with the Master Matsu original.
- **Rap Karma strike** (ep 27): Knocks Mikiko down with Karma during rap training.

## Garry
**Role:** "Green Garry", gang member and Komatsu Norio's student; ally.
- **Karma control** (ep 26): Learned Karma at the disco.
- **Slam** (ep 30): Slams Stevie during dance training.

## Fox Girl
**Role:** Kitsune, Mikiko's former trainer and boss at Kitsune's Farm; ally.
- **Fox martial arts** (ep 26): Trained Mikiko in it, but can't anymore since she lost her fox blood.
- **Throw** (ep 26): Throws Mikiko all the way to Komatsu Norio's disco.

## Kaeru
**Role:** Guardian spirit of the Hekublade, Orenji clan; ally.
**Forms:** Hekubeats (Turns the Hekublade into headphones.)
**Weapons:** Hekublade (she lives inside it)
- **Change** (ep 30): Her KA: changes into anything she chooses.
- **Sound element** (ep 30): Lets Mikiko hear her through headphone form.

## Chase
**Role:** Mysterious co-worker at Kitsune's Farm from Mikiko's world, sent by the old fox king to take the crown; antagonist.
- **Karma control** (ep 28): Knows Karma control and KAs. Even a little of his Karma far outclasses Mikiko's.
- **Crown removal** (ep 29): Took the Fox King crown, which then stopped working for Mikiko.
- **Mind reading (suspected)** (ep 28): Mikiko wonders if he can read minds.

## Technica
**Role:** "Tech", a custom-made Skylander, inventor and Max's wife; ally.
**Look:** Has wings she made with Yuri's help. Smokes.
**Weapons:** Custom wings, Unnamed ranged weapon (shoots at Kill)
- **Scan** (ep 27): Scans Lexy and finds something wrong.
- **Invention** (ep 27): Built her own wings and is making anti Kyle phones with Yuri.
- **Shooting** (ep 30): Shoots at Kill.
- **Flight** (ep 27): Flies with her custom wings.

## Yuri Tenjo
**Role:** Ally at HQ Earth and an inventor.
- **Invention** (ep 27): Helped Tech make wings and anti Kyle phones.

## Kyrie
**Role:** Queen Kyrie of Royal capital Section 1, daughter of Queen Loverie and the late King; ally.
- **Hairstyling** (ep 27): Restyles Rhalix's hair as a disguise.

## SR Leader (Senju)
**Role:** Senju, leader of SR ("Father" to its members); wants a strong Skylander group to take over the Royal capital; main villain.
**Look:** Carries a sword (flashback). SR members wear his hoodie.
**Forms:** Split self (Split into two halves; Kill is the other half.)
**Weapons:** Sword
- **Self-splitting** (ep 31): A demon/devil skill used to split himself in two.
- **Demon granting** (ep 27): Gives demons to members and turns people into demon vessels.

## Keita
**Role:** SR member who calls the leader "Father"; lazy but powerful KA user; enemy. Killed by Rhalix.
**Look:** Wears a KRH (Karma reducing hoodie).
**Weapons:** Sword (cut down Persephone)
- **Everything I say you believe** (ep 27): His KA: anyone who hears him believes and obeys what he says, even loss of sight or hearing.
- **KA disable** (ep 29): Turns off his own KA.
- **Mass kill attack** (ep 30): Killed all the guards with one attack.

## Logan
**Role:** SR member who calls the leader "Father"; once a knight and inside man at the Royal capital; enemy. Killed by Sam.
- **Thunder flash** (ep 29): Lightning strike that knocked off Sam's hoodie.
- **KA** (ep 29): Has a KA he only ever used on Kill and Keita; never shown.

## Kill

**Look:** Has demon wings like someone Max recognizes. Uses a sword.
**Forms:** Senju's other half (Speaks in the SR Leader's voice; looks younger.)
**Weapons:** Sword
- **Devil skills** (ep 31): Has a devil inside him and can use all of its skills.
- **Sword impale** (ep 30): Ran Max through; claims removing the sword kills faster.

## World terms
- **Karma**: The core power system, from the Xeno Warriors' world. Cause and effect: belief that you are right gives good Karma.
- **Good Karma / Bad Karma**: Good Karma means fighting sure you are right; it boosts how much Karma you can put out. A much stronger foe can still win with bad Karma.
- **Unbalanced Karma**: Karma state from fighting unsure of right and wrong; it weakens you.
- **Dark Karma**: Evil-aligned Karma that can be sensed.
- **Karma control**: The ability to use Karma.
- **First stage of Karma**: "Your mental and physical state is the first stage of karma."
- **True empathy**: Mikiko's unique ability: says what he feels and copies others. Overuse is dangerous and can cost him his true self.
- **Soul state**: Condition of a soul; Mikiko's is corrupted.
- **Corruption**: Mikiko's corrupted transformation, tied to his emotions.
- **Demigod**: Mikiko's heritage.
- **C-soul**: Soul-creatures who attack Mikiko and may sense the Hekublade. Mikiko is also a C-soul.
- **KA**: Each fighter's personal signature ability (e.g., Vampire Slice, Everything I say you believe, Infinite loop, Change).
- **KA Release**: Breaking free of an enemy's KA.
- **KA disable**: Turning off your own KA.
- **Element**: Elemental affinity: Gravity (Rhalix), Wind (Max), Sound (Kaeru). Sainea's daughter knows Karma elements.
- **Kshi**: Rhalix's gravity power.
- **Lunar form**: Rhalix's new form.
- **Fox King mode / Fox King crown**: Mikiko's former form, powered by the crown, which only works for a Fox Kin.
- **Fox Kin / fox blood**: Fox people. Fox Girl lost her fox blood and Mikiko is no longer half fox.
- **Fox martial arts**: Fox Kin fighting style.
- **Old fox king**: Former fox king who sent Chase to take the crown back.
- **Orenji clan**: Mikiko and Kaeru's clan, from ancient Egypt; mostly long dead.
- **Murasaki clan**: Rhalix's prestigious clan.
- **Skylanders**: Descendants of angels sent to watch over humanity, who created the moon.
- **Demon Skylander**: A Skylander fused with a demon (Lexy, Kill).
- **Vessel / fusion**: SR's demon hosts; the goal is full fusion of host and demon.
- **Devil**: Being stronger than demons; demons and devils can learn special skills like splitting oneself. Duo is a devil.
- **Sky Islands**: The Skylander homeland.
- **Commoner islands**: Islands where Sintrie lived.
- **Royal capital**: Skylander capital with 3 sections.
- **Section 1**: Kyrie's section, now Rhalix's kingdom, breaking ties with Sections 2 and 3.
- **Section 2**: Its King killed Section 1's King, according to the queens.
- **Section 3**: Kept the queens locked in and marked rescuing Loverie as dangerous.
- **Skylander King**: Overall ruler; Section 1's King disagreed with him.
- **Royal ID**: ID needed to enter Rhalix's kingdom.
- **Royal army / Grade1 Knight**: Section 1's military and knight ranks.
- **SP (Sky Patrol)**: Rhalix's team: Sam, Max/Sintrie, Lexy.
- **SR (Sky Rebellion)**: Senju's group, which aims to take over the Royal capital; members call him Father. The script calls it "the rebellion".
- **SR hoodie**: Seeing a member wear it binds Sintrie to join SR.
- **Karma reducing hoodie (KRH)**: Hoodie that suppresses the wearer's Karma (Sam, Keita, Lexy).
- **Xeno Warriors**: Mikiko's group from another world; took down the ultimate weapon.
- **Ultimate weapon**: Ultima, defeated by the Xeno Warriors.
- **Government**: Traded smartphones to the Royal capital for Skylander tech and information.
- **Smartphones / Kyle phone**: Smartphones from the Government. The Kyle phone keeps Max from saying his daughter's name.
- **Anti Kyle phones**: Being built by Tech and Yuri.
- **Kyle**: Head of C2; betrayed Max's trust; Ai Oni is hunting him.
- **C2**: Government group that works on human modifications, led by Kyle.
- **Duo**: Devil from Ai Oni's world, rumored among C-souls.
- **HQ Earth**: Base where Yuri and Ultima stay.
- **F3**: Place Ai Oni visited.
- **Mountain Village**: Mikiko's home area, attacked by Ultima before.
- **Kitsune's Farm**: Fox Girl's farm and store.
- **Komatsu Norio's disco**: Training ground for Mikiko, Garry and Stevie.
- **Komatsu Norio style of life**: "We fight with our words, not our hands, unless we're sure what we're doing is right—We Fight!"
- **Manifest Greatness**: Mikiko's chant; said with true belief it powers him up.
- **Hekublade (Heku)**: Mikiko's sword, guarded by Kaeru.
- **Hekubeats**: Headphone form of the Hekublade.
- **Healing pod / healing potion**: Healing items. Zane vanished from a healing pod.
- **Skyhater**: Attacker of Skylanders.
- **Year 90**: When Sintrie was recruited by Senju (flashback).
- **Old world**: The Xeno Warriors' original world.
