# Freedom Fighters Recarnet (Season 1): Lore Summary for the Mod

**Coverage: I read 100% of /tmp/Freedom_Fighters_Recarnet_S1.txt, all 3,510 lines (about 150k characters), in order from start to end.**

How complete the source is: EP 1 through EP 8 are fully written. EP 1 also contains crossover scenes from the main series labelled "Xeno Warriors Recarnet Gotcha life Ep 28-31". EP 1 ends with a block of speaker names that have no dialogue (Lexy, Logan, Kyrie, Persephone, Sam, Komatsu, Garry, Kaeru, Ai Oni). EP 9 is only a list of speaker names with no lines. EP 10, 11 and 12 are empty. "EP ??" is a fragment with a few lines, and "Tab 14" is empty. Anything after EP 8 is therefore a placeholder and cannot be used as canon.

---

## 1. Premise and timeline position

- The story starts after the Xeno Warriors fight with Ultima ("the ultimate weapon") in Aurora, which happens at the end of XWR Season 2. The news reports the Xeno Warriors' win and tells the public that karma exists. Most people think it is fake news. A former government worker, "Gov guy 2306", has quit and says it is real.
- Mikiko wakes up after **2 weeks unconscious** from overusing True Empathy. His arm is burned and bandaged. Rhalix now has a **Lunar form** and is away with the **Sky Patrol (SP)**, which is the other spin-off, *Sky Patrol Recarnet*. The two shows run at the same time and share events: the attack on the royal capital, SR, and Rhalix's interview.
- Zane "disappeared in his healing pod".
- Fox Girl sends Mikiko to train under **Komatsu Norio**, a disco dancer. This is the "Komatsu Norio style of life" that the show is built around.
- The main threads are:
  1. Mikiko learns how karma really works (certainty against uncertainty).
  2. A new old-world arrival, Chase, steals his Fox King crown.
  3. Mikiko, Yuri and Ultima become the "Freedom Fighters", with **Gale Manchester Aurora** paying for them, so they can attack government facilities F1 to F5.
  4. **Ai Oni** and her prototype demons plan to capture Mikiko.
  5. Mikiko's corruption grows (a new dark-blade power) and nearly makes him kill someone.
- The background threats are the **Black hole**, the **Keepers** (Mikiko and Rhalix "have 3 years" before they need to worry about them), **Duo** the devil, and **Kyle** (head of C2).
- The timeline wording is inconsistent. Characters say both "2 weeks" and "2 years" since arriving. The narrator says Mikiko "got a phone during the 2 years of training". Fox Girl and Ai Oni refer to a "good timeline" and are slowly getting those memories back. Mikiko has not.

## 2. Characters

### AmenXL Mikiko ("Young Mikikies", "Red Mikikies", "Xeno Warrior")
- **Role:** Main character, leader of the Xeno Warriors, founder of the "Freedom Fighters". Works at Kitsune's Farm and is wanted by Aurora's government. Lives in PineCone City / Mountain Village. His name is AmenXL ("Ah-men-XL"). People kept saying "A-men-XL", so he goes by Mikiko, the name Fox Girl gave him.
- **Nature:** Corrupted soul state (he is a C-soul), Orenji clan blood (the clan comes from ancient Egypt), and a demigod. He is **no longer half fox kin**. Eating Skylander wings boosted his karma and strength and woke up "a new power inside the corruption of my soul".
- **New outfit (EP 4, for the model):** Overalls (Garry's pick, a nod to the farm), an armor pad on one shoulder (Stevie's idea), and a hoodie tied around his waist (Komatsu's idea). He wears goggles when skydiving. He has a phone. Later he wears the invisible F1 tracker pendant. In corrupted form his **arm turns red**, and the red arm can pierce bodies.
- **Powers and moves:**
  - **True Empathy:** Reads emotions and copies others' feelings and moves, such as the Dance Kick and freestyle bars. **Drawback (stated by Komatsu):** overuse makes his soul copy others until he "loses his true self" and can only imitate. Overuse also put him in a 2-week coma. He also uses it to dodge Ultima at 30%.
  - **Fox King Mode:** **Lost in EP 29.** Chase took the crown, and it only works for fox kin. Komatsu says Mikiko's base form is now as strong as old Fox King mode.
  - **Manifest Greatness / "MANIFEST TRUE GREATNESS!":** A chant that charges him and makes him glow red ("XLing").
  - **Netsu / Netsu Bust:** A speed burst. Netsu Bust broke the dojo treadmill.
  - **Xenoblast:** Named in a rap.
  - **Kitsune Attack Tier 1** and **Tier 3:** Fox martial arts strikes. He hid Tier 3 from Ultima's scanning.
  - **Soul Splitter / Soul Return:** Splits his soul into fighting duplicates. Rhalix can make one; Mikiko can make many.
  - **After Shock:** A demon skill. It puts a ball of fire over the target's head as a timed bomb.
  - **KA Release:** Mentioned as costly. He would use it to break Zack's Infinite Loop.
  - **Super jump.** Karma sensing and hiding.
  - **Corrupted (C-soul) form, new dark power:**
    - **Dark Blade summon**
    - **"Scatter":** blades spread out and track karma
    - **"Fall":** a rain of blades
    - **Absorb:** takes his own blades' karma back into his body
    - **"Begone!":** a point-blank dark karma blast
    - Earth-shaking power-up. Yuri's computer measured it at about **3.5x Ultima's 100% output**.
  - **Corruption drawback:** His heart rate climbs until his heart stops. It triggers when he wants or feels like killing. Each transformation makes it more permanent. It is "gonna get him killed" according to Fox Girl.
- **Hekublade abilities (with Kaeru):**
  - **Heku** (the sword): got stronger after his power-up and can send enemies into the blade.
  - **Hekubeats:** Kaeru turns the blade into headphones so they can talk. Her element is sound.
  - **Heku shield:** unbreakable, but the force still goes into his arm.
- **Personality:** Hot-headed, airheaded, refuses to kill, kind. He keeps calling Jacob "Jack/Jake". He learns to rap. Catchphrases: "I fight with my words not my hands ya know!" and the dojo creed. Freestyle rap lines include "never give up, that's the title for me", "hit it with a netsu and then a xenoblast", and "Mi-Kik-o!"

### Komatsu Norio ("Master Matsu" / "Master Matus")
- **Role:** "The Genuine and passionate ultimate disco dancer." Rapper and owner of the **Disco Dojo**. Teaches karma theory, dance, rap and stamina. Raised the Gang Brothers. Teaches "online classes". Trained Zane and "Henery", who left to fight Ultima.
- **Moves:** **The Komatsu Norio Dance Kick** (call: "The Komatsu Norio!" / "Dance Kick!"). Very fast, trips and kicks opponents, broke the treadmill to beat Mikiko's record ("Get it going x9"). "Music flows through my body just like Karma."
- **Creed (repeated):** "We fight with our words! / Not our hands ya know! / Unless, we're sure what we're doing is right! / We Fight! / Ya heard!"
- **Visual cues:** Disco styling. He supplies the "new disco outfits".

### Garry ("Green Garry") and Stevie ("Purple Stevie"), the Gang Brothers
- **Role:** Komatsu's adopted sons. They fought weapon-carrying gangs through rap battles and protected farm animals that gangs had made "reckless". They learned karma one month before EP 1.
- **Visual:** Garry's color is green and Stevie's is purple. Both wear disco-style "drip".
- **Stevie:** Took Mikiko out of Fox King mode in 5 seconds with good karma. A "human calculator" who forgets what comes after 1. Steals his brother's bars.
- **Garry:** Slightly smarter. Slams Stevie.
- **Both:** Karma blasts (they tried to blast open Yuri's HQ). War cry "RARRRR!!!". Comic relief. They kicked a 1%-output Ultima down.

### Fox Girl (Len Kitsune)
- **Role:** Mikiko's adoptive mother. She gave him her blood in the old world. Owner of **Kitsune's Farm**. **She lost all her fox blood.** She made the Hekublade and can summon it. She trains Kaeru with thousands of push-ups.
- Ultima judges her as "a whole different level". He says he couldn't land a hit on her even with every data capsule. She throws Mikiko long distances (her standard way of moving him around) and arrives with a gust of wind. She is getting back good-timeline memories, in which she and Mikiko were closer.
- **Line:** "Don't ever talk back to your mother like that again."

### Kaeru
- **Role:** Guardian of the Hekublade and lives inside it. **Orenji clan.** Calls Mikiko "Mikiko-senpai" and "dummy/dum dum". Calls Fox Girl "Master". Gave up romantic feelings to become a guardian.
- **Element:** Sound. **KA: "Change"**, which lets her change into anything, including the blade, headphones or a shield. **Skills:** Fox Heal (revives near-dead targets) and HekuBlade Scatter. She also starts a move "Heku Bar—" that the script cuts off. Strong enough to cut down corrupted Mikiko once.

### Yuri Tenjo
- **Role:** Hacker and genius with an underground **HQ** that has a VR room. Ultima's little sister. Called "that Green girl" by the brothers, so green is her color. Has a crush on Mikiko. Admits she knowingly put a possibly explosive anti-virus in Mikiko's wrist in S2. Wants to be a Freedom Fighter.
- **Tech:** Camera and elevator hacking, a laptop that conjures beds and VR clones, and "Computer: Open sky ground". Running gag: the "Tenjo Medical technique" ("God loves us… NOW WAKE UP OR ELSE WE'RE ALL GONNA DIE!").

### Ultima (Aniri, Project Ultima)
- **Role:** Yuri's big brother and ex-government weapon, recovering in a "weakened state". He is joining the Freedom Fighters against his will. He only lets Mikiko call him "Aniri" once. He insists Yuri may only date someone stronger than him. He swears constantly, and Jacob's computer is "unable to convert language".
- **Powers:**
  - **Output %:** 15%, 30%, 65% (can hold for 2 minutes) and 100% (now about 3 minutes, down from 5).
  - **Move scanning:** predicts an opponent's next move. He cannot scan Kitsune Attack Tier 3.
  - Senses with his eyes closed, and can hit all soul duplicates at once.
  - **"Stop right there!":** freezes an opponent's movement.
  - Flight, very high speed, and **heart restart ("CLEAR!")**.
- Still chasing data capsules. Kyle probably has the F3 capsule.

### VR Ultima
- Copies of Ultima made by Yuri's computer. Her machine can only run them at 20% output. One copy sacrifices itself and says "Keep fighting.. You can do it.." The system calls out "VR Ultima is down You Win!"

### Gale Manchester Aurora ("Young Gale")
- **Role:** Grandson of the President of Aurora. From the old world, where he was Mikiko's annoying rich classmate. He edited Mikiko out of the Ultima fight videos to keep him from becoming popular. He wants the presidency/throne before his father arrives from the old world. His stated goal is a peaceful Aurora that rewards hard work.
- He funds the "Freedom Fighters Corporation" through the **Gale Empire**. In return he wants votes. He doesn't know what a phone number is.
- **Personality:** "Hohohoho" laugh, drops French ("Excusez-moi", "Oui", "Tellement stupide", "quoi"), calls Mikiko "broke boy", and makes a "dumb face".

### Jacob Matador
- **Role:** Criminal interrogator in a vest Yuri doesn't recognize. He says he "resigned from facility uno (F1)", which is contradictory. Formal speech ("peaceful and formal conversation").
- **Tech:** Raises barriers that Ultima can't break, isolates people one at a time to interrogate them, censors speech through "Computer", and warps people to other places. Mikiko feels karma being used but **senses none from Jacob**.
- Puts a **tracker pendant/crest** on Mikiko. It goes invisible and only switches on inside Aurora. Big fan of Gale. Gets annoyed at being called "Jack".

### Chase
- **Role:** Temporary worker at Kitsune's Farm. Gives one-word answers ("Yeah."). Fought off gangs and bears alone and knows karma control and KAs. He may read minds.
- He is from the old world. The **Old Fox King** sent him to take back the "fake king's" crown. His karma is small in amount but overwhelms Mikiko's. He quits after taking the crown.
- Kaeru's test for whether he is Orenji clan: "Trueeee…" / "depression".

### Ai Oni (Ai and Oni)
- **Role:** A real demon (Mikiko also counts her as one of the "2 devils" he has fought). Master of Zack and Kaitlyn. **Split personality:** Ai (good) wants to be sealed back into Rhalix, and Oni (evil) wants freedom. Their deal is that they are re-sealed only if they see Rhalix, so she avoids him. Ai warns that without Rhalix "the blackhole will kill us all".
- Fully masks her karma. Cuts down Zane twice and one-kicks Mikiko.
- **Plan:** Capture Mikiko, hand him to the government (C2) for experiments, and have Zack upload video. This would lure out **Kyle** and **Duo**.

### Zack
- **Role:** Prototype demon and Ai Oni's student. A runaway human who was gassed into a demon. Seen at F3. His girlfriend is **Atherea**, who calls during the royal capital attack.
- **Fighting:** Knife, demon form, invisibility that also hides his karma, portals, and a move that "took down an SR member".
- **KA: Infinite Loop.** The target keeps repeating their current action and never completes it. It loops back every 5 seconds, and running in place drains stamina. **Drawback:** it only works while Zack himself stays still ("not in momentum"), so he can't move while it runs.
- Becomes uncertain when Mikiko asks "Are you sure what you're doing is right?" Leaves after Kaeru saves Kaitlyn.

### Kaitlyn ("Kate", "Kati")
- **Role:** Prototype demon. Flirty and has a crush on Ai Oni. Poses as a villager calling for help.
- **KA:** Not named. It is supposed to make the capture easy and needs her to raise her hand. It was never landed.
- About 80% of Ultima's max power, the same as Zack. Impaled by corrupted Mikiko's red arm and revived by Fox Heal.

### Zane the Magical Mage
- **Role:** Mikiko's friend. Returns in Ep 31 shouting "ZANE THE MAGICAL MAGE!!!!" and hates being called a nerd.
- Uses **Magic, not karma elements:** Fire, Ice and Thunder spells. He has a **Magical Orb** that holds his **Mana** and the souls and spells of past mages.
- A **KA** lets attacks pass through him. It is a projection; "the real you isn't here". His real body's location is unknown, and "EP ??" says Mikiko can't find him.

### Others
- **SR (royal capital faction):** "SR Leader" ("Father", karma stronger than Mikiko's) and his children Lexy, Logan and Keita. Lexy's "captain" is Rhalix. Kyrie and Persephone are named only in blank lines. **Skylander Sam** appears in a flashback reporting Ai Oni's team fighting "like 20 Kyles".
- **Kyle:** Head of **C2** (government human modifications). Has a KA and probably holds the F3 data capsule. A flashback with **Technica** ("caring for others will get you killed") hints she is dead. An odd voice leads him into the **Keepers' palace**.
- **Duo:** A devil from the old world. The C-souls talk about him.
- **Rhalix:** Away with SP. Has a Lunar form and once caused a "moon fall" that wrecked F3. Gave an interview crediting Mikiko.
- **Henery:** Former dojo member, now gone.
- **Desk worker:** Gale's hotel staff.
- **Guy1 and Guy2:** Aurora fans who volunteer at the farm store.
- **News girl:** Reports the news in EP 1.
- **Master Yo:** Mikiko's past trainer, mentioned only.
- **Old Fox King:** Offscreen.

## 3. Factions and organizations
- **Freedom Fighters:** "Something someone is, not a team." Gale treats it as a corporation. Members are Mikiko, Yuri and Ultima (reluctant). Komatsu's dojo lives by the same idea.
- **Xeno Warriors:** Only Mikiko and Rhalix are active.
- **Disco Dojo** (Komatsu, Garry, Stevie).
- **Gale Empire:** The hotel plus Gale's money.
- **Aurora Government:** Facilities F1 to F5. **F1** is the biggest and uses Sky Tech plus an "unknown force" (karma or magic). **C2** handles human modifications. The government gasses humans into prototype demons.
- **Ai Oni's team:** Runaway prototype demons.
- **SR:** At the royal capital, which is under attack in Ep 28-31.
- **Sky Patrol (SP):** Rhalix's group.
- **Keepers**, the **Old Fox King** and the fox kin.
- **Orenji clan:** Nearly extinct. Mikiko and Kaeru belong to it.

## 4. Locations (build notes)
- **Mountain Village / PineCone City:** Mikiko's home area. Rural, hit by Ultima before.
- **Kitsune's Farm:** A farm-supply store with a register that takes cards, open fields with "dangerous animals" and bears, a greenhouse, and Mikiko's room upstairs.
- **Disco Dojo:** A disco club with a dance floor and a broken treadmill or training area. Build ideas: a light-up dance floor (colored glass and redstone lamps), a DJ booth, treadmills (a moving-belt block).
- **Yuri's HQ:** Underground under Aurora's outskirts. Holds a computer lab and a **VR room** that can reshape itself into an outdoor environment. Has a "sky ground" hatch in the roof.
- **Aurora City:** A city of camera-covered streets with a hidden camera-free path. The outskirts are patrolled. Residents pay by card and live paycheck to paycheck.
- **Gale's Hotel / Manor:** Looks like a house outside and is a large luxury hotel inside. Gale's house sits on top. The hacked elevator launched rooms into the sky. Yuri's crew rebuilt them as **apartments**.
- **Government Facilities:** F1 is the HQ (barriers, teleporters). F2 was destroyed but has underground hideouts. F3 was damaged by the moon fall. F4 is large. F5 is small and suspicious, and it is the next target.
- **Royal capital:** SR's base.
- **Sky Islands:** Visible from extreme height.
- **Keepers' palace:** Empty.
- **Dark portal:** Found in "EP ??", cause unknown.

- **Bears**, and **"reckless" farm animals** made dangerous by gangs.
- **Gangs:** Armed, and fought through rap battles.
- **VR Ultimas:** Training enemies.
- **Government guards and F1 interrogator tech.**

- **Hekublade:** Unbreakable. Has a guardian spirit (Kaeru). Changes into headphones ("Hekubeats") or a shield. Can be summoned and unsummoned with "Kaeru return!" Fox Girl can recall it.
- **Fox King Crown:** Holds fox power for fox kin only. Stolen by Chase.
- **Mikiko's dark blades:** Karma constructs.
- **Zane's Magical Orb:** Stores mana and past mages' spells.
- **F1 tracker pendant/crest:** Turns invisible, active only in Aurora.
- **F1 barrier and warp tech**, and the "Computer" voice interface.
- **Data capsules:** Ultima's power-ups. The F3 capsule is probably with Kyle.
- **Healing pods**, which Zane vanished from.
- **Yuri's laptop:** Hacking, VR spawning, bed creation.
- **Facility maps for F4 and F5**, supplied by Gale.
- **Phone** and **goggles**.
- **Demon gas** used for human modification.

## 7. Arcs and events in order
1. **EP 1 / Ep 28:** Mikiko wakes up and is thrown into the Disco Dojo. Stevie knocks him out of Fox King mode. He learns the karma lesson and the creed, trains dance, rap and speed, breaks the treadmill with Netsu Bust, and Komatsu beats his record. Yuri calls. Komatsu says his basic training is done. He meets Chase at the farm. **Boss beat:** none.
2. **Ep 29:** Chase takes the Fox King crown. Mikiko can't fight him because his karma is bad.
3. **Ep 30:** Lesson that a strong enough enemy can overpower good karma. Hekubeats is created.
4. **Ep 31 (village battle):** Ai Oni's plan is revealed. Mikiko wipes out a C-soul swarm. **Fight: Zack and Kaitlyn.** Rap-battle dodging, Infinite Loop, and Zane's return with magic. Mikiko defeats Zack with the uncertainty trick, After Shock and Soul Splitter. **Ai Oni appears.** Zane's projection survives her, she kicks Mikiko down, and the scene cuts off.
5. **EP 2:** Ultima ambushes Mikiko at HQ. Yuri names Gale as the man who framed Mikiko. **Jacob Matador's interrogation** follows: Mikiko gets the tracker and Yuri confesses she once planned to kill the president. Jacob warps them to Gale.
6. **EP 3:** The elevator hack sends the hotel into the sky. Mikiko and Ultima rescue the residents. Gale's deal funds the Freedom Fighters, and the rooms become apartments.
7. **EP 4:** New outfit and freestyle dance and rap training. Mikiko quits the dojo out of embarrassment. Gale sends the F4 and F5 maps. **Sparring boss: Ultima** at 15%, then 30%, then 100%. Mikiko lands Kitsune Attack Tier 3. Ultima threatens Yuri, and Mikiko blocks the punch.
8. **EP 5:** Ultima and Yuri's secret plan is to push Mikiko into his dark form. Dark blades come out (Scatter, absorb, Begone). **Corrupted Mikiko** reaches 3.5x Ultima's power. SR, Kyle and Ai Oni all sense it. Ai Oni sends Zack and Kate. Fox Girl orders Kaeru to stop him.
9. **EP 6:** Corrupted Mikiko beats 11 VR Ultimas and real Ultima. **Kaeru fights corrupted Mikiko** (Scatter against HekuBlade Scatter, then "Fall"). The Gang Brothers get in the way, and Kaeru falls.
10. **EP 7:** **Zack and Kaitlyn fight corrupted Mikiko.** Yuri's "medical technique" wakes Kaeru. Mikiko's red arm impales Kate. Ultima throws Kaeru in and Fox Heal saves Kate. Ultima restarts Mikiko's heart. Zack and Kate retreat. Fox Girl takes Kaeru and the Hekublade away and tells Mikiko to rest and go back to the dojo.
11. **EP 8:** The farm-store "money thief" mystery. About $5,960 is "missing", but it went through as card payments ($5,000 arrived). Learned along the way: Mikiko's name story, Fox Girl's good-timeline memories, and the Gang Brothers' backstory.
12. **EP 9 to 12 / EP ??:** Placeholders. Hints: another dojo, Ultima, VR, Zack and Kate session; Zane is still missing; worry that the Keepers sensed Mikiko's unmasked karma; the brothers find a **dark portal**.

## 8. Glossary
- **Good / bad karma:** Certainty that you're right boosts karma output. Uncertainty makes it unbalanced, so a weaker person can drop you.
- **"Remove all karma":** Fight on pure physical strength when there's no time to build good karma.
- **"Mental and physical state is the first stage of karma."**
- **Komatsu Norio style of life:** The dojo creed.
- **C-soul / corrupted soul:** Mikiko's state; also the mob type.
- **Demigod.**
- **Prototype demon:** A gassed human.
- **KA / KA Release.**
- **Output %:** Ultima's power level.
- **Data capsule.**
- **Mana / Magic:** Separate from karma.
- **Good timeline:** Memories some characters are recovering.
- **Fox kin.**
- **Guardian:** The spirit tied to a blade.
- **Freedom Fighter.**
- **"Ya heard / ya know":** Dojo slang.

## 9. Mod content ideas (each tied to the doc)

**Playable characters and forms**
- **Mikiko "Freedom Fighter" skin and kit (FFR era):** Overalls, shoulder pad and hoodie around the waist. Fox King mode is removed in this era. Skills: Netsu, Kitsune Attack T1/T3, Soul Splitter (several clones that end with a Soul Return burst), After Shock (a delayed fire bomb on the target), and Manifest Greatness (a red-glow buff). Ultimate: **Corrupted dark blades** (Scatter homing blades, Fall blade rain, absorb blades back for karma, Begone blast). Super-ultimate (KA-style slot): **True Empathy** (copy a nearby mob's or player's last skill, or dodge with perfect timing).
- **Kaeru as a companion or playable character:** Sound element. KA "Change" in the super-ultimate slot (turn into a block or mob disguise, a shield, or headphones). Fox Heal (revive a downed ally), HekuBlade Scatter.
- **Komatsu Norio as a playable or trainer NPC:** Dance Kick, karma-free physical strikes, and a music-zone buff for himself and allies.
- **Gang Brothers (Garry and Stevie) as a duo NPC companion:** A RARRR charge and a karma blast.

**Bosses**
- **Ultima sparring boss (VR room):** Phases at 15%, 30%, 65% and 100% output, with a timer for each. A scanning mechanic means repeating the same skill gets dodged. A "Stop right there" root. Spawns VR Ultima adds.
- **Corrupted Mikiko:** A boss or a challenge mirror. His power grows on a heartbeat meter, and the fight ends when "his heart stops" if you don't win fast.
- **Zack and Kaitlyn duo boss:** Invisibility that also hides karma, a knife combo, portal blinks. **Infinite Loop:** the player is snapped back to where they stood 5 seconds ago and drains stamina while Zack stands still. Hit him to break it. Mikiko's "Are you sure what you're doing is right?" can turn into a debuff skill that stuns.
- **Ai Oni (late boss):** Karma-masked so she doesn't show on the HUD lock-on. Ai and Oni personality phases.
- **Chase (story event or boss):** He takes the crown. In mod terms, he steals a buff or item from the player.
- **Jacob Matador (non-combat or puzzle boss):** A barrier cage, interrogation dialogue, warp.

**Mobs**
- C-soul swarms drawn to the Hekublade. Prototype demons (gassed humans, two variants with demon forms). Reckless farm animals and bears near the farm. Rap-battle gang members (an "insult" debuff). VR Ultima training dummies in 20% versions. F1 interrogator guards with barrier tech.

**Structures**
- **Disco Dojo:** Light-up floor, DJ booth, treadmills.
- **Kitsune's Farm** with store, greenhouse and Mikiko's room.
- **Yuri's underground HQ** with a VR room arena that changes biome when activated.
- **Gale's Hotel:** A house outside, a tower inside, and Gale's house on the roof.
- **Aurora City outskirts** with camera blocks that alert guards.
- **Government facilities:** F4 is large. F5 is a small decoy that is bigger underground. F1 is the endgame, with barrier and warp pads.
- **Dark portal** structure. **Keepers' palace** (empty, eerie).

**Items**
- **Hekublade** with transform modes (sword, Hekubeats headphones that grant comms or a music buff, unbreakable shield).
- **Fox King Crown:** Works only with a fox-kin flag, otherwise inert.
- **F1 Tracker Pendant:** A curse item that pings guards inside Aurora.
- **Zane's Magical Orb:** Stores mana and casts Fire, Ice and Thunder.
- **Data Capsule** (F3 one is with Kyle). **Facility map items (F4, F5).**
- **Gale Empire funding:** A currency or shop system where Freedom Fighters missions pay out.
- **Disco outfits** as cosmetic armor. **Goggles.**

**Quests**
- "The Komatsu Norio Style of Life": Dojo training covering dance rhythm minigame, treadmill speed test and rap call-and-response. The reward unlocks a good-karma mechanic.
- "Fox King No More": the Chase event.
- "The Unexpected Interrogation": Jacob, with dialogue choices.
- "Gale's Empire": the elevator-launch rescue parkour, then a deal that unlocks funding.
- "The Ultimate Sparring Match", followed by "Corruption Unleashed".
- "Mysterious Money Thief": a farm-store mystery with clues (register, cards, declined payments).
- "Defend the Mountain Village": a C-soul wave, then Zack and Kate.
- "Find Zane / The Dark Portal": a hook for future content.

**Systems**
- **Good/Bad Karma state:** A buff or debuff to karma output based on player "certainty". Examples: attacking a mob that hit you first counts as justified and gives good karma. Attacking passive or neutral mobs or fleeing gives bad karma. A **"Remove Karma"** toggle makes your attacks pure physical damage, which counters bad-karma debuffs. Komatsu's caveat should still apply: good karma only raises output, and a much stronger enemy still wins.
- **Corruption meter** for Mikiko: rises when he lands killing blows. High corruption gives powers plus a heart-rate danger. Each transformation raises the minimum the meter can drop to.
- **True Empathy overuse meter:** Copying too much makes you lose access to your own skills for a while, matching the "losing your true self" drawback.

## 10. Conflicts and things to check against existing systems
1. **Fabric or Forge.** The brief says Forge 1.20.1. The project description and memory say **Fabric** 1.20.1. Confirm which one.
