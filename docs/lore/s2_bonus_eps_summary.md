# Xeno Warriors Recarnet: Season 2 Bonus Episodes (backstories)

**Coverage: 100% read.** The doc has 3 written episodes (the Kyle, Zack and Ava tabs). The "Govemnet" tab (Gracie, Euphoria, Kyle, Persephone, Ava), "Tab 4" (Aniri and Yuri Tenjo) and "Tab 6" (Fox Girl, Chase, Zack, Natturo, Cindney) contain speaker names only, with no lines yet.

## Ep: "The Weapon of Will" (Kyle, Year 85)
- **Brinta Williams (Ms. Mcgotney), Kyle's mother.**
  - She was a government worker who quit just before a promotion to president (she could have overthrown Aurora) when she became pregnant.
  - She lives in Aurora's underclass city.
  - She repeatedly put Kyle up for adoption so he would kill each adoptive family and bring her their money. She hacked the adoption records to keep him off the grid.
  - Her philosophy: "Your will is your greatest weapon. Sharpen it to break others' wills and use them as weapons."
- **Jacob Matador's first day** as a criminal interrogator. Brinta reads his emotional tactics and throws him out.
- **The Aurora virus:**
  - A lifelong sickness, usually present from birth until 18. It killed Kyle's father at 23.
  - Its symptoms are a cough, passing out and trouble breathing.
  - There is no cure except becoming a demon, and that tech was still early (risk of losing your mind).
- **Writer's note:** Brinta stole government tech to move the virus from Kyle into herself. Kyle let her die without giving her the medicine ("If you need medicine to live then you're useless").
- **Kyle joins the government** through the draft test. He got some questions wrong on purpose to land in **C3** (demon work), in case the virus came back.

## Ep: "The Order of Assassins" (Zack, Year 90)
- Zack's parents were killed and their savings stolen. He suspects his **adopted brother**, which hints at Kyle.
- He joins the **Order of Assassins**, a group split into small cells around Aurora that only kills criminals and pays well. His cell is **Chase** and **Cindney** (a Skylander with wings who goes to school on the sky islands).
- **Chase:**
  - "A good assassin can hides his will to kill": his karma and killing intent are hidden.
  - He has a sword and doesn't train people.
  - He uses **Soul Snatch** (a Keepers' move) and **"Begone"**.
  - He is from the old world, like Fox Girl.
- **Natturo** (a Skylander) hires the Order to kill an old farm owner in the Mountain Village / Pine Cone City, claiming he keeps slaves. She was lying because she wanted the land.
- **A Distortion** opens at the farm:
  - C-souls pour out of it and take over people's bodies.
  - Once a C-soul gets inside you, there's no saving you.
  - Soul Snatch pulls them back out.
  - A high-level distortion means someone nearby has huge karma.
- **Fox Girl** arrives with a pink-haired fox tail, kills every C-soul and breaks the Distortion with leftover **divinity** (no sensable karma). Chase knocks Zack and Natturo out to talk with her privately.
- Zack becomes callous over time. Chase leaves his cell.
- **Kaitlyn (Kate):** she and Zack become close friends over late-night talks. She likes girls. Her father abused her mother. They argue loudly, and the argument becomes a comfort for both.
- **Government recruiter "3602"** hands out demon-program cards. The program means 5 years of body training, and demons are immune to all sickness. Zack and Kate sign up for power.

## Ep: "The Friendship of Ava and Persephone" (Year 90)
- **Ava:**
  - Poor, and sick since birth with the Aurora virus. Her body is building a tolerance to her medicine.
  - Her mother **Felsina** is a cage fighter who keeps losing to an opponent with a "strange ability". Her father **Jareal** takes travel jobs.
- **Persephone:**
  - Rich and from Aurora City; Ava is her only real friend.
  - Her **sister was killed by a demon**.
- **Kyle** (on break, a government worker) hands Ava a demon-volunteer invitation, saying demonization cures all sickness. He recognizes her virus.
- Ava's parents forbid it ("demons are soulless monsters"). She runs away.
- Persephone asks Kyle to make her a demon too, so she can protect Ava. Kyle plans to use "my new skylander friend" to prepare them. Both girls follow him.
- **Link to the main series:** Persephone later lives inside Lexy as the voice in her head (from the Sky Patrol doc).

## Mod content ideas
- **Aurora virus status effect:** coughing particles, short blackouts (screen fade) and lowered stamina. It's caught near Aurora/C2 areas. Medicine items from Gov facility loot slow it down. Demons and Ultima are immune.
- **Distortion event:** a rare world event, a portal structure that spawns C-soul waves which possess villagers.
  - Close it by killing the waves, or with a divine or Soul Snatch move.
  - Bigger distortions spawn near high-karma players.
- **Order of Assassins:** a hidden cell base in Aurora (dark room, flyer board) that offers criminal-hit bounties. This feeds straight into the bounty boards.
  - Chase is an NPC whose karma is hidden from lock-on and karma sense.
  - Cindney is a Skylander assassin NPC.
- **Government demon recruiters ("3602"):** NPCs in the city who hand out demon-program cards (a flavor item). They are a hook for the C3 prototype-demon mobs.
- **Underground fight arena** (Felsina): a PvE fight-ladder structure where one opponent has a hidden trick.
- **Soul Snatch / "Begone":** Keeper-style moves for a future Chase boss and as Rhalix's execution.
- **Kyle boss notes:** "weapon of will" (breaks the player's will = karma debuff), hides intent, survived the Aurora virus.
