## 1. Premise and Timeline Position

- **When:** after Season 2 of Xeno Warriors Recarnet, alongside Freedom Fighters Recarnet. Characters talk about "that big fight with Ultima" as something that already happened. Ultima is now a friendly side character who helps build Technica's wings. Lexy is still held by the Sky Rebellion (SR). The old King of Section 1 (Arling) has been killed, and **Rhalix is the new King** of the Royal Capital.
- **Premise:** this is a slice-of-life plus mission series about Rhalix's first months as King. He and his Sky Patrol (SP) crew (Sintrie/"Max", Sam, Queen Kyrie, and later Ongaku and Technica) deal with:
  - a poisoning plot,
  - learning to act like a king,
  - fixing the neglected Commoner Islands,
  - freeing Skylanders who are auctioned and enslaved on the ground levels.
- **Series threads it builds on:**
  - the SR holds Kyrie's mother and Lexy in the City of Aurora,
  - Kyle and the Government are still a threat,
  - Rhalix has to fight "the black hole" again later,
  - in reincarnation ("Recarnet"), people who died *before* the black hole do not keep their memories.
- **Big political change:** Section 1 formally **breaks away from Sections 2 and 3** (Ep 6) and becomes its own kingdom. That lets Rhalix make large reforms.
- Ep 12 is a **flashback set in "Year 90"**: young Sam is evicted from the Commoner Islands under King Arling.

## 2. Characters

### Sky Patrol core

**Rhalix Murasaki** (King of the Royal Capital, Captain of Sky Patrol, former Royal Knight)
- **Background:** orphan. His mother was sent to research a planet in "the old world" when he was about 1 and was killed when it was attacked. A demon killed his father when he was 3, and the army raised him. He "used to have a demon within him", which causes **dark karma** when his emotions run high. He calls himself a **demigod**. His other timeline selves talk inside his head as **"Alix"** and **"Rha"**. He was once banished by the Skylanders and has promised to forgive and protect them. He is smarter than most Skylanders.
- **Look:** retractable wings (he hides them on the ground out of habit). **Eyes glow bright red** when he is enraged, and dark karma surrounds his body. His hand glows **purple** for Soul Snatch. As King he switched to an outfit that looks like **Kyrie's late father's** and is "good for battle". His temporary crown is **Kyrie's own Queen crown**. He ordered a permanent crown that is a **crown with a mask that drops down from it**, meant to "strike fear into enemies". His fighting stance is one hand at the chin, one hand at the side, feet diagonal.
- **Weapons:** **wires** that turn into any weapon (switching them takes a little karma). He also fights well barehanded.
- **Elements:** **Gravity** (purple gravity orbs; can push gravity outward to shake the area) and **Moon** (a "trait element": he creates moons of any size and they **make nearby allies stronger**).
- **Skills/moves:**
  - **Kishi:** his gravity crushes enemies into the ground at any strength. He keeps it on passively, and when he is sick it fires on its own, even from a sneeze.
  - **Soul Snatch:** rips out a target's soul and destroys it, used as an execution.
  - **Karma Activation:** forcibly wakes up someone's dormant karma. It can kill unless a second user helps.
  - **Karma sensing:** finds and tracks people by their karma.
  - **Enhanced hearing:** an example of a karma enhancement.
  - **Lunar/Solar form:** he unlocked a power-boosting form through meditation.
  - **Fast healing.**
- **KA:** none yet. He calls himself "still a memory chaser" (Ep 4).
- **Personality:** loves fighting and training, blunt, dense about romance, still acts like a knight (cooks for himself, eats as if heading into battle, slouches, dodges duties). He cares fiercely for his people and has broken his emotionless knight training. Loves pineapple-and-ham pizza and hates mushrooms. **Catchphrase:** "My Royal Capital!" (everyone uses it).
- **Relationships:** he and Kyrie become a couple in Ep 11. He adopts **Ongaku**.

**Queen Kyrie** (the new Queen of Section 1; Rhalix's love interest)
- **Family:** her father (the old King Arling) was killed. Her mother is **Queen Lovery**, now held by the SR. Athrea and Ranrea are her step-sisters.
- **Weapon:** **rapier** (sword). Rhalix later teaches her to box, and she copies his stance and style perfectly.
- **Element:** Gravity, with **Kishi**. She uses gravity to fetch objects and can stop bullets mid-air.
- **Personality:** fashion-focused, flustered around Rhalix, terrifying when angry. Her signature punishment is "jail **WITHOUT ANY MILK**". She once threw her sword at Rhalix's head for being late. Loves chocolate with peanut butter. Her favorite dish is **BBQ beef kebabs**.

**Sintrie, publicly "Max"** (Vice Captain; promoted to **General** of the Royal army)
- **Background:** a former member of a commoner **thieves organization** with Tatsu and Peri, who stole from royals. He keeps his real name hidden in the Capital. Kyrie teases him as "Maxine". He knows the black market. Rhalix doesn't fully trust him yet because of an earlier lie. He is tracking down the SR bases.
- **Look:** puts on a hoodie for undercover work. Skips the tie on his suit.
- **Weapon:** **dagger/knife**.
- **KA:** **Vampire Cut** (Blood Manipulation, close range). Wounds it makes won't heal, and he takes the victim's health for as long as he keeps it active.
- **Element:** **Wind**, unlocked in Ep 9 under the moon. It blew away bullets.
- **Path:** **Lunar**. Meditating at night sends him into a "mind state".
- **Personality:** sarcastic and loyal. Bad with kids. Breakdances when Rhalix says "break it down".
- **Relationships:** proposes to **Technica Matanodo** in Ep 7 with an **ancient Skylander soul-link bracelet**.

**Sam, real name Dylan Midori** (Executive Officer; 15 years old)
- **Background:** at age 5 he was evicted from the Commoner Islands, dropped to the ground levels by Maddie (SR), and grew up alone (Ep 12). His older sister is **Cindy (Cindeny)**. He was the first in the crew to learn karma. He felt overlooked by Rhalix until Ep 2.

- **Element:** **Clear Space**, a very rare element only Skylanders get. He teleports straight across the space to whoever he is looking at. At first he can't control it and teleports into walls.
- **Signature move:** **Ticking Time Bomb (TTB)**, a bat strike.
- **Personality:** loud and hot-headed, hates meditating. **Catchphrase:** "beat some heads!" (as a kid: "Dylan the great head beater"). Calls himself "Detective Sam". Has to drink apple juice because the Skylander drinking age is 16.

**Lexy, real name Maynaya** (Sky Patrol member, currently held by the SR)

Her **eyes turn red** when she snaps, and she breaks a table with one punch. The SR trains her by force and threatens to "dispose" of her.

## 8. Glossary

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Karma** | Energy anyone can unlock. Good karma means full strength, bad karma weakens it, depending on your moral compass. Advanced uses: enhanced sight and hearing, sensing and tracking, putting karma into weapons and punches, karma auras, karma blasts. |
| **KA (Karma Ability)** | "Manipulation" of something that already exists, such as blood, karma or matter. Not everyone gets one. There may be millions of kinds. |
| **Element** | "Creation": your karma produces the element itself. Everyone eventually gets **2**, decided by personality, birth traits or soul attribute (moon is a "trait element"). Unstable when first unlocked. |
| **Memory chaser** | Someone who hasn't unlocked a KA yet. |
| **Karma Activation** | Jump-starts dormant karma. Risky. |
| **Lunar / Solar path** | Meditating by day or by night shows your path and can unlock a power-boost form. |
| **Angel Eye** | A rare Skylander ability to foresee actions. |
| **Dark karma** | Karma driven by emotion, with red eyes, tied to Rhalix's demon past. |
| **Skylanders** | Angel descendants. They speak every language, process information and heal quickly, have wings, and growing wings is "the second most painful thing" they can go through. Slower to master karma than humans. |
| **Sky Haters** | Humans who hunt Skylanders for their wings. |
| **Sections 1/2/3** | The Skylander kingdoms. |

| **Royal Sickness** | A poison-induced illness with fever, loopiness and uncontrolled elements. |

## 10. Conflicts with Existing Mod Systems

1. **KA drawbacks and burnout:** the doc never mentions drawbacks, a worsening cost, or burnout for any KA (Vampire Cut, Karma Dampener, Matter Manipulation, Telekinesis). The closest thing is Ongaku being exhausted and losing control. You will need to invent drawbacks; Ongaku's exhaustion and instability is the obvious base for hers.
2. **Rhalix's KA:** the doc says Rhalix **has no KA yet** ("still a memory chaser"). If the mod gives him a KA in the super-ultimate slot, that contradicts this doc. It would need to be locked to a later season or explained another way.
3. **Elements don't fit one category:**
   - "Clear Space" is called an element, but it works like teleportation.
   - "Moon" is a trait element that buffs allies.
   - Kishi is a skill of the Gravity element.
   - Everyone gets **2 elements**.
   - Angel Eye and Lunar/Solar forms are **new systems** that are neither a KA nor an element.
   - The doc says humans unlock karma, KAs and elements faster than Skylanders and that some people never get a KA, which matches your rarity setup.
4. **Karma drain:** Kiara's KA zeroes the karma bar, and Tech uses no karma at all. This needs extra handling for Ultima's Data and for a tech-based meter.
5. **Good and bad karma:** strength depends on your morals. This could tie into a reputation stat, but no such system exists yet.
6. **Season lock:** Ultima appears friendly, which means this content comes after Season 2. Sky Patrol content should sit behind the Season 2 or Season 3 unlock.
7. **Government:** in this doc the Government shows up only indirectly (Tech's permissions, the Servitude link, Section 3's deal). There are no Government facilities, guards or pods, except the pod Tech came out of.
8. **Mod loader:** the task says Forge 1.20.1, but the project description says **Fabric** 1.20.1. Worth confirming.
9. **Mature content:** sky weed, smoking, drinking, an innuendo gag, and swearing should be left out or toned down.
