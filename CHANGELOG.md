# Changelog

## 0.147: Combo combat
- **Universal combo combat** for players and party bots. Each weapon has its own string of hits:
  - fists: 5
  - daggers: 5
  - swords: 4
  - axes and hammers: 3
  - spears and tridents: 4
  - tools: 3
- **Timing like Epic Fight.** Each hit has a wind-up, the next one starts at a set point, and early clicks are queued.
- **Finishers and heavies.** Finishers knock enemies away. Sneak + click is a heavy launcher that also breaks shields.
- **Stagger keeps strings connected.** After 9 hits in 3 seconds the target can't be staggered for 2 seconds. Bosses only flinch from big hits.
- **Balanced against vanilla.** Damage is worked out from each weapon's attack speed, so modded weapons balance too.
- **Epic Fight's battle mode** takes over combat when it's on.
- **Mikiko's fist string:** 2 punches, then Bajiquan (stomp punch, elbow, rising palm, double palm), ending in **Tie Shan Kao**. It gets wild and sloppy below half health.
- **Garry's beatbox:** new head-bopping beatbox idle, and his mic stays in his hand.

## 0.146: Auras
